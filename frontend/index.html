01THE CONTEXT

Evidence review brings together details that deserve a clear structure

Collection, packaging, storage and custody each add context to an evidence record. When those details are spread across notes and forms, it can take extra effort to see them together.

ForenSight offers a consistent place to record reported conditions and review preliminary indicators, while leaving interpretation with the people doing the work.

A

Many related conditions

Evidence handling involves multiple attributes, from collection through storage and documentation.

B

Information needs context

A recorded condition is one part of a broader review, not a conclusion on its own.

C

Uncertainty should stay visible

Unknown or unrecorded details are kept explicit rather than filled in by assumption.

02ABOUT FORENSIGHT

Built to explore structured, human-led evidence assessment

ForenSight AI is an AI-assisted forensic evidence risk-assessment platform and decision-support prototype. It was developed to explore how evidence-related information and handling conditions can be recorded and reviewed in a consistent interface.

The workspace captures user-provided evidence details, presents preliminary rule-based risk indicators and recommendations, and keeps experimental machine-learning outputs separate from the primary assessment.

03HOW IT WORKS

A straightforward path from documented conditions to informed review

The current workflow records the information supplied by the user, applies the existing preliminary rule-based assessment, and presents the result for human interpretation.

  1. 01

    Enter evidence information

    Record a reference, evidence type and the handling conditions available to you.

  2. 02

    Review the reported context

    Collection, PPE, tools, packaging, storage and custody details are assessed using the existing rules.

  3. 03

    See preliminary indicators

    The workspace displays the resulting risk category, score, contributing factors and precaution recommendations.

  4. 04

    Interpret with human expertise

    Use the record as a prompt for further review, not as a definitive forensic conclusion.

06TRANSPARENCY & LIMITATIONS

A prototype, with its limits in plain view

ForenSight supports exploration and structured review. Its outputs are not validated forensic findings, and should not be used as definitive conclusions.

  • Synthetic dataModel training and evaluation use synthetic data, not verified forensic outcomes.
  • Rule-based primary assessmentThe preliminary rule-based score is the main screening mechanism; it is not confirmation of contamination.
  • Experimental classifiersML results are exploratory and do not establish real-world performance.
  • Human expertise remains essentialIndependent verification and qualified interpretation are necessary.
  • No independent validationThe platform has not established legal or forensic validity through independent validation.
